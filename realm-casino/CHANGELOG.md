# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — Beta, fortune and crossings

- Added Plinko with twelve independent rows, thirteen payout bins, a published 92.1142578125% theoretical gross return, an animated ivory ball and a two-block cabinet with 78 protruding brass pins.
- Added the Fortune Wheel with twenty equally likely sectors, a published 95% theoretical gross return, a crowned gilded stand and a matching animated wheel menu.
- Added Royal Crossing: guide a miniature chicken across up to ten medieval roads, with an 80% success chance per crossing and a choice to collect or continue. A crash loses the wager; ten successes automatically collect.
- Added three original craftable blocks to the creative tab and search, matching owner/rules/play interfaces, Portuguese and English translations, motion/sound preferences and tactile game sounds.
- Extended the existing Chronicles RP wallet journal, fully funded bank reservations, persistent pending prizes, replay protection and optional Realm Notifications to all three new games.
- Royal Crossing progress and its complete maximum liability persist across restart. Closing, leaving, disconnecting or reaching the sixty-second deadline automatically collects the earned value without another random draw.
- Added exact rational cent rounding and casino data format 2, with automatic reading of existing format-1 worlds. All six games retain server-owned outcomes and physical block access.
- Further games and Crown Ledger casino taxation remain planned.

## 0.1.0 — Beta, phase one

- Added three original medieval casino blocks: a two-block slot cabinet, a European roulette table and a blackjack table, with crafting recipes, creative search entries, gold trim, carved wood and velvet.
- Added three-reel slots with a published 95.3125% theoretical return, single-zero roulette, and single-player blackjack against a funded house.
- Added animated reels, roulette and cards, matching owner/rules/play menus, sounds and client sound preferences, in English and Portuguese.
- Players own, name and fund their machines; wager limits stay within server limits. Owners cannot control outcomes or probabilities.
- Integrated the Chronicles RP virtual wallet. Without Chronicles or a created character, economic actions remain disabled; no alternate currency is created.
- Added bank liability reservations, persistent pending payouts, protected funded blocks, server-owned results, physical sessions and replay protection.
- Added a durable transfer journal with player receipts, safe recovery and failure blocking; blackjack hands persist and automatically stand on disconnection, closure or timeout.
- Added optional Realm Notifications results and payment messages. Crown Ledger taxation and further casino games remain planned.
