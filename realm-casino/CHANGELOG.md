# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — Beta, phase one

- Added three original medieval casino blocks: a two-block slot cabinet, a European roulette table and a blackjack table, with crafting recipes, creative search entries, gold trim, carved wood and velvet.
- Added three-reel slots with a published 95.3125% theoretical return, single-zero roulette, and single-player blackjack against a funded house.
- Added animated reels, roulette and cards, matching owner/rules/play menus, sounds and client sound preferences, in English and Portuguese.
- Players own, name and fund their machines; wager limits stay within server limits. Owners cannot control outcomes or probabilities.
- Integrated the Chronicles RP virtual wallet. Without Chronicles or a created character, economic actions remain disabled; no alternate currency is created.
- Added bank liability reservations, persistent pending payouts, protected funded blocks, server-owned results, physical sessions and replay protection.
- Added a durable transfer journal with player receipts, safe recovery and failure blocking; blackjack hands persist and automatically stand on disconnection, closure or timeout.
- Added optional Realm Notifications results and payment messages. Crown Ledger taxation and further casino games remain planned.
