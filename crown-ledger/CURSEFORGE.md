## Description

Borrow from the kingdom through the new **Royal Credit Counter**: an original medieval block with a ledger, parchment, quill, coins and wax seal. Apply for a loan, review the President's fixed-interest offer and sign to receive the principal in your existing wallet. Pay manual installments or settle the full agreed balance early at any counter. Each agreement shows its complete repayment schedule and recorded decisions.

Public money is reserved before an offer can be signed. Loan payments return to the treasury, and interest stays fixed even when an installment is overdue. The current sworn President approves applications within administrator limits. Emerald credit is supported without Realm Origins. Breaking a counter preserves the world's contracts.


Give your kingdom a shared treasury and let its citizens take part in public decisions.

Realm Treasury, formerly Crown Ledger, brings a craftable medieval treasury chest, public donations, configurable taxes, funded salaries and public contracts. The elected, sworn President can announce secret yes/no votes about any topic from the chest. Write a custom title and a multiline description to explain the proposal; citizens can read the full text before voting and revisit it in the decision history. Citizens receive a discreet on-screen notice and press F8 to open their ballot, without a forced menu interrupting gameplay.

Approve treasury-funded benefits for crop growth, mining, hunger and experience, or hold free advisory polls. One citizen gets one vote, which can be changed before the deadline. Participation requirements, strict-majority approval and a cooldown keep decisions orderly. Ties reject the proposal. Rejected proposals return their reserved money to the treasury.

Prices, limits and durations are set by the server administrator. Benefits never pay the President personally and cannot spend money already reserved for another purpose.

Optional integrations include Realm Council, Realm Origins / Chronicles RP, Contracts & Bounties, Realm Markets, Realm Casino, Laws & Justice, Realm Economy, Realm Marriage and Realm Notifications. Without Realm Council, an operator can appoint the treasury steward. Without the Chronicles wallet, the treasury supports emeralds.

Works in singleplayer, LAN worlds and dedicated servers. Install the same version on the server and every client.

Updating from Crown Ledger: replace its JAR with Realm Treasury. Existing treasury accounts and integrations are preserved. Both use the same internal mod ID, so install only one of them. Back up your world before the format 9 migration.
