# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — Kingdom crafting research

- Adds Realm Progression as a separate, optional Realm Treasury addon for Minecraft 1.21.1 and NeoForge.
- Detects server-installed mods and provides a physical administrator menu with search, independent euro goals, namespace scope, recipe coverage, explicit confirmations and audit records.
- Starts with a permanent, kingdom-wide Create crafting unlock at €8,000 of available public euro funds. Unlocking never spends the money.
- Guards actual crafting outputs in inventory crafting, the Vanilla crafting table and the Vanilla automatic crafter, including alternative recipe IDs, stale results, Shift crafting and automatic recipe caches.
- Confirms saved Treasury balances, observes committed income and stores approvals atomically before synchronising or announcing them.
- Preserves unlocked states through spending, target increases, world restarts and temporary mod removal.
- Adds an original 3D Research Table, crafting recipe, drops, creative tab, translations and a parchment/royal-blue interface.
- Shows queued royal unlock banners and explanatory item tooltips.
- Restricts configuration to server-authorised administrators and protects essential systems.
- Supports singleplayer, TCP LAN and dedicated-server architecture with world-wide persistent state.
- Documents the crafting scope explicitly: custom machines, loot, trades and existing-item use require separate treatment and are not claimed to be globally blocked.
