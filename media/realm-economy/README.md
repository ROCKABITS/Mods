# Realm Economy — imagens

[Download dos 27 PNGs](0.4.0/realm-economy-0.4.0-media.zip) · [Galeria completa](0.4.0/README.md) · [Guia](../../realm-economy/README.md)

Capturas reais de Minecraft 1.21.1/NeoForge. A construção, as personagens, os fundos e os materiais são demonstrações. Os menus e as operações financeiras são os de produção, com carteira Chronicles e tesouraria Crown reais. O período de beacon do cenário foi configurado em 10 segundos para verificar a expiração; o padrão é 600 segundos.

## Mesa da Economia

![Original Economy Desk](0.4.0/realm-economy-desk-close.png)

Suggested title: **The Economy Desk**. Caption: An original medieval desk with an open ledger, quill, inkwell, brass fittings and coins. Craft it to consult kingdom prices and manage the economy.

## Preços do reino

![Governor prices and taxes](0.4.0/realm-economy-governor.png)

Suggested title: **Govern the kingdom economy**. Caption: The sworn President sets prices and service taxes within administrator bounds. Every change records the previous and new policy.

## Limites administrativos

![Administrator economic bounds](0.4.0/realm-economy-admin-bounds.png)

Suggested title: **Administrator bounds**. Caption: Set minimum and maximum prices, tax ceilings, paid-system switches and beacon periods.

## Bigorna com euros

![Anvil paid quote](0.4.0/realm-economy-anvil.png)

Suggested title: **Pay euros, keep your XP**. Caption: Review the base price, public tax and total before repairing, combining or renaming. Original materials remain necessary.

![Paid enchanting](0.4.0/realm-economy-enchanting.png)

## Poções

## Histórico público

![Crown economic receipts](0.4.0/realm-economy-crown-history.png)

Suggested title: **Taxes for the public treasury**. Caption: Service taxes reach Crown Ledger with durable receipts. The base price leaves circulation and the President never receives the tax personally.
