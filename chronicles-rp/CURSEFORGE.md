# Chronicles RP

Summary: Fantasy roleplay characters with public RP names, Origins lineages and classes, a parchment character book, and digital-to-physical euro currency.

Create an RP character with a custom name, age, occupation, backstory and hand-drawn signature. Start on a mysterious parchment character creator beside your actual player skin. Click its lineage button to choose an Origins race, followed by the optional Classes selection, then return to the same RP draft. Reveal your starting fortune through an animated destiny roll.

Press P to open the character book. View identity, Origins class, reputation, rank, achievements, play time, history and wallet. Its Origins button opens lineage, class and powers information and returns to your book or unfinished character draft. Server datapack origins are supported through Origins' own registries.

Your created character's RP name appears above your head, in TAB, in the author name of ordinary Minecraft chat messages, and in your Chronicles RP book. Players without a created character keep their normal username. Only the public name and UUID are shared for this display; private profile details are not broadcast. Account identity and command targets remain unchanged. RP names do not have to be unique. Other mods may still display account usernames if they read them directly.

Use digital euros or withdraw physical inventory coins and banknotes, deposit them with exact change, and pay other players. Eight coin denominations and seven banknotes range from one cent to €500. Origins class, RP occupation and starting fortune are independent.

Chronicles RP supports English and Portuguese. Origins and its add-ons provide their own lineage/class translations. Reputation, missions and medals are managed by operators.

Requires Minecraft Java 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.256+ (21.1 branch), Java 21, Origins (NeoForge) 0.3.2 by IAFEnvoy, and Jupiter for NeoForge 1.21.1. Origins: Classes (NeoForge) 0.3 is optional. Install the same mod set on server and clients.

Updating from 0.4.1 preserves existing RP details and balances. Lineage follows the selected Origins origin. Back up your world and replace the previous JAR. Network protocol 7 requires Chronicles RP 0.5.3 on the server and all clients; do not mix it with 0.5.2. No character reset or economy conversion is required.
